# The problem

The connection had roughly 10 Mbps downstream and 500 Kbps upstream, shared by around 15 devices. Large transfers competed with games, calls, browsing, and streaming. The project aimed to preserve responsiveness during simultaneous use, not to increase DSL sync speed.

The initial latency figures are owner-reported history, not measurements reproduced during repository preparation. Good results meant the household could keep using the connection during a game and a call, with less disruption from uploads. A speed-test score alone did not capture that goal.
